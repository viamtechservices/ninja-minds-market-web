About DRONIAC

A drone education initiative by NinjaMinds.

We help school students learn applied technology through drone making, electronics, sensors, flight testing, and responsible flying.

Our Story

Built on nearly a decade of hands-on STEM education.

DRONIAC is a venture owned and managed by NinjaMinds. We provide NEP-compliant extracurricular skill development courses aimed towards technology, applied learning, and practical innovation.

Before DRONIAC, our team worked as MINDGRIND PVT LTD with more than 22 schools across Himachal Pradesh and Jammu. With nearly a decade of experience in STEM education, robotics training, school workshops, and hands-on learning programmes, NinjaMinds was founded to create deeper, more practical technology learning experiences for students.

DRONIAC is our focused drone education initiative designed to help school-going children understand drone making, drone flying, electronics, sensors, flight logging, and real-world drone applications.

Our Mission

To turn curiosity into practical mastery.

To help school students learn technology by building, testing, flying, failing, improving, and finally mastering real drone systems.

Learn by Doing

Students handle components, make decisions, and solve practical problems with mentor support.

Build with Purpose

Every build connects electronics, design, calibration, and application into one complete system.

Fly with Responsibility

Training emphasizes control, records, safety, discipline, and respect for flight environments.
